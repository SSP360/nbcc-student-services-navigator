# Escalation Policy

## Purpose

This policy defines when and how the application escalates student inquiries to human support.

## Escalation Triggers

The application must escalate (redirect to human support) when:

1. **Sensitive Personal Information**: Student describes mental health crisis, financial hardship, discrimination, or safety concerns.
2. **Academic/Financial Decisions**: Student seeks advice on program selection, course prerequisites, financial eligibility, or loan approval.
3. **Accessibility Accommodations**: Student requests personalized accommodations or accessibility assessments.
4. **Unmatched Query**: No approved source addresses the student's question.
5. **Urgent Timeframe**: Student indicates an urgent deadline (registration closing, exam date, etc.).
6. **Source Error**: Source retrieval fails; human verification required.

## Escalation Process

1. **Acknowledge**: Display a message confirming the student's question.
2. **Explain**: State why the question requires human support.
3. **Route**: Provide the appropriate NBCC contact method (phone, email, office location) from source NBCC-SS-007 (Contact NBCC).
4. **Record**: Log escalation reason for Day 2+ case management (deferred).

## Contact Routes

Routes are defined in `knowledge/sources.yaml` source NBCC-SS-007.

- **General enquiries**: Main contact centre
- **Academic support**: Student Success Coaching (NBCC-SS-002)
- **Accessibility needs**: Accessibility and Inclusion Services (NBCC-SS-004)
- **Mental health/wellness**: Wellness and Counselling (NBCC-SS-005)
- **Financial support**: Financial Aid office (contact via NBCC-SS-007)

## Deferred

- Automated ticketing system (Day 2+)
- Case history tracking (Day 2+)
- Escalation follow-up (Day 2+)
